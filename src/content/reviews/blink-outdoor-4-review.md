## Quick verdict

Blink Outdoor 4 is a battery-powered camera to evaluate for outdoor motion awareness in an Alexa-based setup. Blink's FAQ documents a required Sync Module and distinguishes local recording from subscription features. This is document-based guidance, not a physical evaluation or a guarantee of detection reliability.

## Specifications and setup notes

The key buying question is not just video quality; it is whether the Blink app, Wi-Fi coverage, battery workflow, and Alexa routines fit how you actually monitor your home. Confirm camera placement before buying: outdoor cameras need a strong enough signal at the install location, not just near the router.

Blink documents night vision, two-way audio, and Alexa integration. Person Detection requires a subscription or eligible trial. Local storage uses Sync Module 2 or Sync Module XR with separate storage media; do not assume every module bundle offers that option. Google Home and Apple Home integration were not established by these sources.
