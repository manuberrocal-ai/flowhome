## Quick verdict

Eufy Indoor Cam C120 is an indoor-monitoring candidate when local storage and supported assistant integrations matter. The manufacturer documents local and cloud recording options; that is not a promise that every recording feature or cloud service is free. This review is document-based guidance, not a physical evaluation or a security certification.

It is still an indoor camera, not a whole-home security system. The best fit is a buyer who wants a compact app-controlled camera for specific rooms and is willing to think carefully about placement, privacy, and notification settings.

## Specifications and setup notes

The manufacturer lists night vision, two-way audio, and Apple HomeKit, Google Assistant, and Alexa integration. Its HomeKit instructions require activation through the eufy app, and video is limited to 1080p in HomeKit. The consulted page is European: confirm the US model, firmware, storage media, and service terms before purchase. Current prices and customer ratings were not verified.

Setup should start with placement, not the app. Decide what the camera should monitor and what it should never see. Avoid bedrooms, bathrooms, and sensitive workspaces unless everyone in the home clearly agrees. For shared homes, camera placement is a privacy decision as much as a technical decision.

## Pros and cons

The useful documented distinction is a choice of recording destinations alongside assistant integration. The tradeoff is configuration: storage, notification behavior, and HomeKit operation must be checked for the exact intended setup, not inferred from a single compatibility badge.

Privacy, notification tuning, and account access remain ownership responsibilities. The manufacturer marks this camera non-waterproof; do not choose it for exposed outdoor coverage. It is not a replacement for an alarm or monitored security system.

If those choices are unresolved, defer the purchase rather than assuming the camera will suit the household. Check current regional service terms and storage accessories separately from the camera price.
