## Setup and compatibility

Plan the charging location and the paths the robot will need through the home. Keep cables, small objects, rugs, thresholds, and furniture spacing in mind during setup. Connect the robot to Wi-Fi, then confirm that the app workflow and preferred voice assistant fit the household. Alexa and Google Home are both listed as compatible, but the profile does not define every command or automation available in each ecosystem.

Because PrecisionVision obstacle avoidance and Imprint Smart Mapping are separate capabilities, it is useful to configure rooms and routines deliberately after the initial map is created. Review the first several scheduled runs for cords, pet bowls, light floor coverings, and areas where traffic changes frequently. That setup work does not prove performance in a specific home; it simply helps identify whether the available controls fit the intended routine.

## Strengths and tradeoffs

The documented mapping, obstacle-recognition approach, and self-emptying configuration are useful comparison criteria. They do not establish an avoidance success rate or eliminate the need to inspect floors. The original j7+ is vacuum-only; the manufacturer's pairing with a separate Braava mop should not be mistaken for built-in mopping. App dependence, dock space, and consumable availability are additional ownership considerations.
