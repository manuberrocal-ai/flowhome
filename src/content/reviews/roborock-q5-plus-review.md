## Quick verdict

The original Roborock Q5+ is a candidate when mapped vacuuming and dock-based emptying are priorities. Roborock's [official Q5 page](https://us.roborock.com/pages/roborock-q5) identifies Auto-Empty Dock Pure support, PreciSense Precision LiDAR, 3D Mapping, and custom routines. This is a document-based assessment of the vacuum-only model, not a physical cleaning test, a reliability measurement, or a ranking of all robot vacuums.

## Specifications and setup notes

Setup depends on the floor plan as much as the app. Start by measuring the intended dock area and checking thresholds, rugs, cables, pet bowls, and low furniture. The Auto-Empty Dock Pure needs a practical location with access to power and room for the robot to return. Wi-Fi and app control can support scheduled cleaning, room naming, zones, and custom routines after the map is established.

PreciSense Precision LiDAR is the navigation approach documented for this model. Roborock also describes 3D Mapping, which can help shoppers understand how the system is intended to represent rooms and cleaning areas. That is useful context for planning, not a guarantee that a particular layout, obstacle, or dark corner will be handled identically in every household. The product profile does not document an obstacle-detection field for this model, so shoppers should not infer one from mapping terminology alone.

## Setup and ownership notes

Before buying, think about floor type, thresholds, rug edges, pet hair, furniture spacing, and where the charging area will live. The robot needs a realistic path through the home, and the dock needs enough clearance to remain accessible. App mapping and room naming are worth doing carefully because they can make scheduled cleaning and zone cleaning more useful. Revisit the map after furniture moves or a change in room layout rather than assuming the first setup will remain ideal forever.

The [Q5+ manual](https://support.roborock.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/5983654392089) instructs users to supervise the entire first cleaning route and use a secure physical barrier when operating in a raised area. Follow the manual's preparation and dock-clearance instructions; a saved map is not a substitute for those precautions. Confirm replacement consumables, bag availability, and support terms before purchase.

## Buying advice

Shortlist the original Q5+ for its documented mapping and emptying-dock configuration, then verify the specific app or voice commands you need.
